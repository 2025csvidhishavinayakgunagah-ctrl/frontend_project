About Karnataka

Karnataka is a state in southern India known for its rich history, beautiful landscapes, ancient temples, waterfalls, wildlife, beaches and vibrant culture. From the heritage of Hampi and Mysuru to the hills of Coorg and Chikkamagaluru, Karnataka offers many experiences for travellers.

📍 Famous Tourist Places

Mysuru

Famous for Mysuru Palace, heritage, culture and Dasara celebrations.

Hampi

A UNESCO World Heritage Site famous for Vijayanagara architecture and monuments.

Coorg

Known for coffee plantations, forests, hills and scenic landscapes.

Gokarna

A coastal destination known for beaches, temples and beautiful sunsets.

🏛️ Historical Places

Hampi

Hampi contains remarkable ruins and monuments from the Vijayanagara Empire.

Badami

Famous for its rock-cut cave temples and Chalukyan architecture.

Pattadakal

A major heritage site known for its historic temple architecture.

Mysuru Palace

One of Karnataka's famous royal landmarks and a major tourist attraction.

⛰️ Waterfalls & Hill Stations

Jog Falls

One of Karnataka's well-known waterfalls, surrounded by the Western Ghats.

Chikkamagaluru

A scenic hill destination known for coffee plantations and mountain landscapes.

Kemmanagundi

A beautiful hill station surrounded by greenery and mountains.

Hebbe Falls

A scenic waterfall located in the Chikkamagaluru region.

🍛 Famous Karnataka Foods

🥞

Masala Dosa

Crispy dosa served with spicy potato filling, chutney and sambar.

🍚

Bisi Bele Bath

A traditional rice and lentil dish prepared with vegetables and spices.

🍩

Mysore Pak

A popular sweet made using gram flour, ghee and sugar.

🍛

Ragi Mudde

A traditional Karnataka dish made from finger millet flour.

✈️ Travel Information

🚆 By Train

Major cities such as Bengaluru, Mysuru, Hubballi and Mangaluru are connected by rail.

🚌 By Bus

Karnataka has extensive road connectivity between cities and tourist destinations.

✈️ By Air

Bengaluru and Mangaluru are among the major airports serving travellers to Karnataka.

🏨 Accommodation

Travellers can choose from hotels, resorts, homestays and other accommodation options.

Check official Karnataka Tourism information for current travel guidance, accommodation and tourism services before planning your trip.
